The pitch

A week that doesn't feel like a week.

We start in southern Cebu where you swim with whale sharks, then slide down a turquoise canyon to a waterfall, then float inside a tornado of millions of sardines.

From there we fly to Palawan, where the limestone cliffs of El Nido rise straight out of the sea and we kayak into hidden lagoons. Two distinct vibes — adventure first half, postcard second half.

Real talk: it's the first week of the wet season. We'll get afternoon rain a few days. Mornings are usually clear and that's when we'll be doing the big stuff anyway. Trip cost works out to roughly $2,500–3,500 per person all-in.

El Nido / Tour C
Day 06 · Friday

The secret beaches.

Tour C goes harder than Tour A — fewer crowds, wilder snorkeling, the iconic "squeeze through the rock to find a hidden beach" moments. The most photogenic day of the trip.

9:00 AM
Helicopter Island
Named for its shape. Long white-sand beach, crystal water, basically empty.
11:00 AM
Hidden Beach
A horseshoe of sand completely enclosed by 80m cliffs. You wade through a slot in the rock to find it. Surreal.
1:00 PM
Matinloc Shrine viewpoint
Climb 5 minutes up jagged limestone for the best view in El Nido. Bring shoes, the rock is sharp.
3:00 PM
Secret Beach
Another hidden cove. Snorkel through a tunnel in the rock, emerge into a beach you can only reach by swimming.
DUSK
Sunset cocktails at The Birdhouse
Treehouse-style bar above Marimegmeg Beach. Last full night.

Real talk · weather + logistics

Things you should know about June.

I checked all of this. June is technically the start of the wet season, but it's not a deal-breaker — you just have to plan around it.

  • 01
    Cebu in June: ~14 rainy daysMostly afternoon showers. Mornings are usually clear, which is when we'll be doing whale sharks, sardines, and canyoneering anyway. Sea is 86°F, the warmest of the year.
  • 02
    Kawasan can close after heavy rainThe slot canyon floods if it rained hard the night before. Operators monitor it daily. We build a "rain day" buffer into the schedule and check the official Badian MDRRMO Facebook page each morning.
  • 03
    Kawasan now requires advance bookingNew "no booking, no entry" policy as of 2026. We book through an accredited operator who handles all permits.
  • 04
    El Nido Tours run mornings, rain or shineBoats go out in the AM almost every day. Even if afternoons get wet, the lagoons are unaffected.
  • 05
    Pack: dry bag, rain jacket, reef-safe sunscreenSkip a hard suitcase, bring a duffle. Reef shoes are useful for canyoneering (rentable on-site for ~$3 if you don't want to carry them).
  • 06
    AirSwift flights book upCebu → El Nido is the direct option but only AirSwift flies it, ~2 flights/day. Lock in seats as soon as we commit to dates.
